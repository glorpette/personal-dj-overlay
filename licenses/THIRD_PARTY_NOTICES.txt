Spout2 interoperability
-----------------------
The native helper implements the Spout sender-map and Direct3D interoperability
protocol without bundling the Spout SDK or its binaries. If code is later
copied from Spout2, retain the BSD-2-Clause notice from:
https://github.com/leadedge/Spout2/blob/master/LICENSE

Optional FFmpeg download
------------------------
The --install-ffmpeg command downloads a pinned SHA-256 archive of the
win64 LGPL build from BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds. The archive is not embedded in the
executable. Its license and copying files are retained beside the installed
binary in %LOCALAPPDATA%\vdj-live-overlay\tools\ffmpeg\licenses.
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
